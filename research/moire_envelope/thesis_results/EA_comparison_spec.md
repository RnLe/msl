# Envelope Approximation Validation Runs Pipeline

This document details the exact parameters and specifications for computing the Envelope Approximation (EA) pipeline to validate against direct FDFD/MPB supercell diagonalization data. We have three target regimes (8°, 3° and 1°). 

Ensure the following outputs are produced and exported correctly for all cases listed below:
1. `lambda_n(R,k)` shift-grid periodic local band potential
2. `v_mn(R)` effective velocity / drift operator terms
3. `M_mn(R)` inverse mass tensor and higher degree geometric data
4. Moiré potential `A_j(R)` (Berry connection components)
5. Eigenvalues of the resultant EA continuum equation solved for $N_{modes}$ modes at the supercell $\Gamma$ point.

## Global Material & Lattice Parameters
- **Lattice**: Square
- **Polarization**: TM
- **Dielectric Profile**: Rods in vacuum
  - $r/a = 0.2$
  - $\varepsilon_{\text{rod}} = 8.9$
  - $\varepsilon_{\text{bg}} = 1.0$

---

## Benchmark Case 1: 8.17° (Large spacing, Fast variation)
*Current reference: Direct MPB & FDFD data calculated at `sigma=0.02` with 30 modes.*

- **Twist Angle**: $\theta \approx 8.17^\circ$
- **Supercell Vector Parameters**: $(M, N) = (14, 1)$
- **EA Target Modes**: 30 lowest TM modes at $\Gamma$

## Benchmark Case 2: 3.01° (Intermediate Moiré cell)
*Current reference: High-resolution MPB/FDFD multi-resolution sweep at `sigma=0.02` with 50 modes.*

- **Twist Angle**: $\theta \approx 3.01^\circ$
- **Supercell Vector Parameters**: $(M, N) = (38, 1)$
- **EA Target Modes**: 50 lowest TM modes at $\Gamma$

## Benchmark Case 3: 1.00° (Extreme Envelope Limit)
*Current reference: FDFD "envelope-scale" `res=1 px/cell` calculation evaluated at `sigma=0.02` with 50 modes. (Conventional MPB collapses here, so this case isolates EA versus the FDFD effective-medium)*

- **Twist Angle**: $\theta \approx 1.005^\circ$
- **Supercell Vector Parameters**: $(M, N) = (114, 1)$
- **EA Target Modes**: 50 lowest TM modes at $\Gamma$

## Instructions for Developer
Run the EA subscale generator and compile the effective continuous Hamiltonian $H_{\text{eff}} (\mathbf{R})$ using the shift-grid technique. Please output final eigenvalues as `.npz` files (e.g., `ea_gamma_modes_8deg.npz`, `ea_gamma_modes_3deg.npz`, `ea_gamma_modes_1deg.npz`) mapped into identical physical arrays using comparable shift matrices to overlay against the pre-existing arrays generated by our direct solvers.
